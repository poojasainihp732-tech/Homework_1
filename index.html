Introduction

The magic of Dr. Satinder Sartaaj and his songs.

Dr. Satinder Sartaaj is a Punjabi singer, songwriter, actor and poet who really heals our hearts with his melodious voice; it is really amazing. Everyone feels good and calm when they listen to Sartaaj's songs. We said that Sartaaj has magic who can proud with everyone heart and mind with his songs. For example: His songs, which are famously known as "sai", means a beautiful Ibadat (worship) to God, realizing his good and bad things. But the words of his songs are really nice. One of his famous song is "Udarian", which was really outstanding. He represents the beauty of Punjab in his songs and represents it in a beautiful appearance.

Satinder Sartaaj won many prizes and noble awards due to the respect for his songs and writing. In his words, he can indulge the Sufi concept that brings the best and good songs to the audience. He played harmonium and tabla for his concerts; his shows became a blockbuster. Honestly, we all know that he is one of the most celebrated and beloved artists in the history of contemporary Punjabi tradition and a Sufi philosopher who can claim a great position rather than other artists. He is very hardworking and punctual in his work. Each and everyone feels proud because he represents himself as the best singer not only in India, but in London, Ontario and Sydney as well.

Music and Achievements

Dr Satinder Sartaaj's poetry is celebrated for its deep knowledge and Sufi, philosophical wisdom. As we know, he is a very romantic artist and many of his songs, which he wrote himself, express his love and deep romance in a pure form. Magical memories such as "Rukh Bolan Laggde Ne", "Jad Zikr Tera Hove" explore devotion and present captivating ideas that made his songs such as outstanding masterpieces. Due to his deep knowledge of Sufi and Punjabi singing, one of his most popular songs is "Rutba", which represents that we can meet with love and spend time, there is no harm of everyone's self-respect. His acting career is really awesome because he plays deep roles and proved himself as a poet with deep, meaningful thoughts.
